Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder and wait a minute. You can press "Send a new code" after 30 seconds. Codes expire after 5 minutes.

My account is locked

After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 5 minutes to protect your account. Wait, then try again.

My stats are wrong

Stats are rebuilt from every ball scored. If a ball was entered wrongly, ask the scorer to undo it; your stats correct themselves.

How do I join a team?

Create a player account, then ask your club admin to add you to their team.
